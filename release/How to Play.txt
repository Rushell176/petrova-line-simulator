PETROVA LINE SIMULATOR v0.1.0

Requirements:
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A DirectX 12, Vulkan, or OpenGL-capable GPU with current drivers

How to start:
1. Extract this ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Double-click "Petrova Line Simulator.exe".
3. Click inside the game window to capture your mouse.

Controls:
- Mouse: Look around
- W A S D: Move
- Space: Move up
- Shift: Move down
- P: Toggle the lights
- Click the projector while aiming at it: Toggle the lights
- F11: Toggle fullscreen
- Esc: Release the mouse / close the game

The projector starts OFF. Press P or aim at the projector and click to turn it on.

This is a fan-made scene recreation inspired by Project Hail Mary.
